\section{Experimental Setup}
\label{sec:exp}

\subsection{Frozen forecast contract}
The main task is \textbf{96 hours of hourly history $\rightarrow$ 72 hours of future forecast}.
The target is \texttt{water\_level\_m}, which is \emph{already} the non-tidal
residual: the database satisfies the exact identity
\texttt{water\_level\_m} $+$ \texttt{tide\_level\_m} $=$
\texttt{observed\_total\_water\_level\_m} to machine precision
(MAE $=0.00000000$~m, $r=1.000000$, verified by
\texttt{tests/test\_target\_semantics\_contract.py}). The residual must therefore
never be de-tided a second time; an earlier pipeline that computed
\texttt{water\_level\_m} $-$ \texttt{tide\_level\_m} injected a spurious
oscillatory component and its checkpoints are discarded.
The historical pilot (Issue~\#6) used 72h$\rightarrow$24h on de-tided residuals only;
that model is classified as exploratory and \emph{must not} be compared directly
against main-task metrics.

Five regions are admitted: \texttt{ny\_bight}, \texttt{thames\_north\_sea},
\texttt{southeast\_australia}, \texttt{us\_gulf\_coast}, \texttt{south\_atlantic\_charleston}.
Hong Kong (\texttt{pearl\_river\_hong\_kong}) is excluded pending license/datum/signoff.

\subsection{Split protocol}
Two split protocols appear in this paper's experiments, and all current
claims use the first.

\paragraph{Operative: frozen calendar split (Section 3.4).} All five-region
expanded evaluations, the architecture-interaction analysis, the
mechanism probes and the six-arm physics ablation use the pre-registered
calendar protocol: 2015--2020 for fitting, 2021 for epoch selection,
2022--2024 touched once. Splits are event-grouped with a 168\,h embargo at
both boundaries, scalers and tail thresholds are fitted on training rows
only, and the 2022--2024 test period is disclosed as retrospective
(Section 3.4); it is not an untouched confirmatory holdout.

\paragraph{Historical: event-grouped 8:1:1 split.} The candidate-zoo
experiments (the four-generator production benchmark, the Wave 1.7 audit
aggregates and the 320-test family) used an event-grouped chronological
8:1:1 split over a single test year. A forecast origin $o$ is safe only if
$o \geq a + 96$ and $o \leq b - 72$, where $[a, b)$ is the partition's row
block, so no window's support $[o-L, o+H)$ crosses a partition boundary.
Aggregates from this protocol are tagged \emph{historical} wherever they
appear and are never merged with the frozen-protocol results.

\paragraph{Shared guarantees.} Under both protocols: train-only scalers
(mean/SD fit on train rows only, applied without re-fit), train-only
thresholds (q95/q99 from train rows only, used for tail metrics and
oversampling weights), and missing masks (all models consume a boolean mask
per hour, fixed and common across all A0--A4 methods).

\subsection{Comparison groups}
The primary analysis compares, per region and per forecast architecture, a
real-only baseline and two conditional-generator augmentation arms, each
under a matched downstream training budget:

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{A0}: Real-only training, no augmentation (baseline).
    \item \textbf{A3}: Standard conditional Flow Matching augmentation;
    synthetic windows are appended to the training set at a fixed ratio.
    \item \textbf{A4}: Tail-aware conditional Flow Matching augmentation
    (the primary H1 arm).
\end{itemize}

Each arm is crossed with a GRU and a TCN forecaster, giving four
augmentation-versus-baseline comparisons per region (A3--GRU, A3--TCN,
A4--GRU, A4--TCN) and a primary family of 20 comparisons over five regions.
Earlier internal waves additionally explored an oversampling arm (A1) and a
noise/phase-shift perturbation arm (A2) in the historical candidate zoo;
those arms are not part of the pre-specified primary family and appear only
in the preserved historical analysis (Section~\ref{sec:results-augmentation}).
Oversampling and history-noise controls return in the bounded tail-calibration
study (Section~\ref{sec:results-tail-calibration}) as exploratory arms.

\subsection{Multiple-testing families}
\label{subsec:families}
Every family below was declared before its test; no family is pooled with
another, and all p-values come from the audited machinery of
Section~\ref{sec:results-augmentation} (exact sign-flip enumeration for
$\le$16 units, otherwise a 10{,}000-draw Monte Carlo with the resolution
floor $(b+1)/(n_{\text{boot}}+1)$):

\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Primary}: 20 augmentation-versus-baseline comparisons,
    Bonferroni $\alpha = 0.05/20 = 0.0025$.
    \item \textbf{Architecture interaction}: 10 paired TCN-minus-GRU
    contrasts (5 regions $\times$ 2 arms), Bonferroni
    $\alpha = 0.05/10 = 0.005$.
    \item \textbf{Physics ablation}: 20 primary mechanism contrasts (5
    contrasts $\times$ 2 regions $\times$ 2 architectures), Bonferroni
    $\alpha = 0.0025$; arm-versus-standard contrasts are secondary and
    exploratory.
    \item \textbf{Regional heterogeneity}: Cochran--Q per augmentation arm
    (4 tests) plus Holm-corrected pairwise contrasts (10 per arm).
    \item \textbf{Severity dependence}: 20 Spearman correlations
    (5 regions $\times$ 4 arms), Holm-corrected.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Reproducibility metadata}
Every run records exact git commit SHA, data file hashes, split manifest hash,
random seed list, optimizer parameters, checkpoint SHA, and wall-clock time.
Generator seeds are $\{42, 99\}$ (per-unit deltas are seed-averaged before
testing, with per-seed spread recorded); downstream forecaster seeds are
$\{42, 99, 7\}$, averaged per cell. An earlier five-seed list was declared
but never realised in the augmentation grid; that discrepancy is one of the
defects corrected in Section~\ref{sec:results-augmentation}. All generative
checkpoints in the benchmark are verified against their declared
architectures: tensor counts, generator-only versus auxiliary parameter
counts, and SHA256 digests are recorded per cell, and a strict validator
rejects any missing, extra or corrupt cell
(Section~\ref{sec:results-generators}).

\subsection{Hardware and run environment}
GPU0 is prohibited throughout the programme. The Wave 2.7 recovery studies and
the physics ablation ran on CPU with explicit thread caps
(\texttt{torch.set\_num\_threads($\le 8$)} and
\texttt{CUDA\_VISIBLE\_DEVICES=""}); heavy jobs are strictly serial (a
concurrent-job incident triggered a systemd-oomd kill, recorded and
corrected). The five-region expanded recompute used a single spare GPU, and the
season-stratified benchmark refresh
was run CPU-only under its fairness contract. One historical DDPM pilot,
retained only for the diagnosis in Section~\ref{sec:results-pilot}, executed
on one RTX~3090 GPU; its CPU reproduction confirmed the
magnitude failure using the same checkpoint loaded with
\texttt{map\_location="cpu"}. Checkpoints are preserved locally and never
committed to Git; their SHA256 digests are recorded in the result artifacts.
