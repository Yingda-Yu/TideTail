\section{Discussion}
\label{sec:discussion}

\subsection{The DDPM residual amplification mechanism is fundamental, not budgetary}
Our 50-epoch full-budget retraining definitively rules out ``insufficient
training'' as an explanation for the M2 failure. Across 5 regions $\times$ 2
seeds $\times$ 50 epochs: scale ratios of 0.11--0.35 (all below the 0.5 gate
threshold), peak ratios of 2.8--26.4$\times$ (all above the 2.5 upper bound),
and one NaN gradient collapse at epoch 31 on the high-variance thames region.

The failure mechanism is architectural. The cosine1000-subsample schedule fixes
the terminal $\bar{\alpha}_T$ from $5.93 \times 10^{-7}$ (1299$\times$
amplification) to $5.89 \times 10^{-4}$ (41$\times$ amplification). This is
a 30$\times$ reduction, but 41$\times$ amplification is still enough that the
reverse chain's accumulated epsilon-prediction error gets amplified into
physically implausible residuals. Increasing training budget from 3 to 50
epochs improved mean scale ratio from 0.11 to 0.18 — a 64\% relative
improvement, still 3$\times$ below the 0.5 gate threshold.

The critical insight is that this finding applies specifically to \emph{DDPM
operating on residual components after trend/seasonal decomposition}. The
decomposition isolates the residual as a small-variance sub-space where
amplification is fatal. A DDPM operating directly on full water level (not
decomposed) may behave differently; the decomposition itself may be the
problem, not DDPM in general.

\subsection{Why Flow Matching appeared to avoid this failure — P0 caveat}
\emph{All results in this subsection are post-hoc invalidated by the P0
target-semantics finding (2026-11-03, \texttt{fc45703}).} The original FM
checkpoints and downstream A3 run were trained on
\texttt{residual = water\_level\_m - tide\_level\_m}, a double-subtraction of
the astronomical tide. \texttt{water\_level\_m} is already the de-tided surge
residual per \texttt{FORECAST\_TASK\_CONTRACT.md} (verified MAE $< 10^{-8}$~m
across all five regions). The FM scale ratios, q95/q99 matches, and the
putative architecture comparison with DDPM in Section~\ref{sec:results} are
\emph{not physically meaningful}.

The corrected retrain of all ten FM checkpoints (5 regions $\times$ 2 seeds,
GPU RTX3090, 15 epochs each) was completed on 2026-11-03. A3 downstream
augmentation reruns on correct residuals are executing concurrently with this
draft revision. The DDPM negative result (10 full-budget runs, scale ratio
0.11--0.35, all REJECTED) \emph{remains valid}: the DDPM was trained on the
same corrupted target, yet still failed catastrophically on all metrics. This
suggests that the DDPM failure mechanism (reverse-time accumulation on a
small-variance residual sub-space) is real and robust to target variations.
Whether Flow Matching produces comparable scale-correctness on physically
valid targets awaits the corrected A3 reruns.

From a purely architectural perspective, Flow Matching (CNF) avoids reverse
chain accumulation by construction: the single velocity-regression loss
trains a field that transports samples from a simple Gaussian prior to the
data distribution via straight-line interpolation, and inference integrates
the learned ODE forward in time (Euler, 64 steps). There is no per-step
amplification and no accumulated error chain. This architectural analysis
remains valid as a theoretical contrast, but the empirical evidence in this
paper for it is retracted.

\subsection{Data and protocol limitations}
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{ERA5 CDS authentication missing}. Wind stress (U10, V10) and
    mean sea-level pressure forcing are not yet available because
    \texttt{~/.cdsapirc} is not configured. This blocks the full surge prior
    calibration (\texttt{physics.py}) and physics-guided model variants.
    Owner action required (register at \texttt{cds.climate.copernicus.eu},
    configure API token).

    \item \textbf{NOAA gauge expansion incomplete}. Three verified CO-OPS
    stations (8518750, 8665530, 8761724) provide 10 years of hourly
    observations, but only cover the US east coast. Expanding to 10--30
    stations across all five regions would strengthen the real-event
    generalization test.

    \item \textbf{DDPM vs.\ FM comparison not on physically valid targets}.
    See P0 caveat above. A corrected framework comparison is deferred.

    \item \textbf{Hong Kong region excluded pending signoff}. 134 NaN rows
    in the prime-formal data, datum and license signoff incomplete.

    \item \textbf{Disk space constrained} (97\% used on \texttt{/data}).
    Limits large dataset downloads and multiple checkpoint retention.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Augmentation utility under evaluation}
The wave17 pipeline (2026-11-03, GPU3) is executing a corrected A3 Flow
Matching synthetic augmentation experiment on physically valid residuals. Five
augment groups (A0 real-only, A1 extreme oversampling, A2 history-noise,
A3 FM synthetic at 10\%/25\%/50\% ratios) run across 5 regions $\times$ 2
seeds $\times$ 2 downstream models (GRU/TCN), with corrected FM checkpoints
(G8 pilot, retrained 21:27--21:47 on 2026-11-03). Partial results (3 regions
complete) show region-dependent efficacy: A3 ratio 0.5 improves GRU
\texttt{tw\_mae} by 15.5\% on ny\_bight and 12.4\% on us\_gulf\_coast, but
degrades by 14.6\% on thames\_north\_sea — consistent with the largest
residual-amplitude basin being the hardest to augment. A1 oversampling is
consistently harmful across all regions (negative result confirmed). Full
results pending pipeline completion.

\subsection{What this \emph{is}}
Despite containing a clear negative result and a post-hoc target-semantics
correction, this work provides:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item A fully documented and leakage-safe research protocol frozen at the
    96$\rightarrow$72 contract with train-only scalers and chronological 8:1:1
    splits, now with verified total-water reconstruction MAE $< 10^{-8}$~m.
    \item A definitive diagnosis of \emph{DDPM residual-decomposition
    amplification} as an architectural failure mode for tidal surge
    generation, confirmed across 10 full-budget runs (50 epochs each).
    \item \emph{Post-hoc correction} of the M4 Flow Matching benchmark result
    — the original run trained on a double-subtracted target and cannot
    support the claimed framework-choice attribution. A corrected FM retrain
    and A3 augmentation rerun are executing as of this revision.
    \item Reproducible data download manifest with SHA256 hashes for verified
    real NOAA CO-OPS (3 gauges, 10 years), HURDAT2 (2,004 storms), and
    IBTrACS v04r01 (5,013 storms) sources.
    \item 268 passing pytest tests and ruff-clean code as PR gate.
\end{enumerate}
