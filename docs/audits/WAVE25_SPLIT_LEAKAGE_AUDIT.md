# Wave 2.5 — Split Leakage Audit

**Date**: 2026-11-04
**Auditor**: TideTail Wave 2.5
**Scope**: Verify train/val/test split integrity across all three protocols.

## Executive Summary

All three split protocols are **leakage-free** within their own definitions. However, **existing checkpoints trained on the primary split (2015-2022 train) CANNOT be reused for expanded_3yr_test (2022-2024 test)** without retraining, because 2022 appears in both.

## Split Protocol Definition

| Split | Train | Val | Test | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **primary** | 2015-2022 (8 yr) | 2023 | 2024 | ✅ Clean, no overlap |
| **expanded_2yr_test** | 2015-2021 (7 yr) | 2022 | 2023-2024 | ✅ Clean, no overlap |
| **expanded_3yr_test** | 2015-2020 (6 yr) | 2021 | 2022-2024 | ✅ Clean, no overlap |

## Window Index Verification

Per-region `window_index_v1.npz` files were verified to correspond to the **primary split**:
- Train windows: 69,970 spanning 2015-2022
- Val windows: 8,600 spanning 2023
- Test windows: 8,601 spanning 2024

**train_years ∩ test_years = ∅** (confirmed for ny_bight, us_gulf_coast — all 5 regions use identical split logic).

## Leakage Risks and Resolutions

### Risk 1: Expanded Test Years Overlap with Training Data

**Problem**: All 50 existing checkpoints (FM × 2 variants, VAE, TimeGrad, Diffusion-TS) were trained on 2015-2022 data. The expanded_3yr_test protocol puts 2022 in the test set.

**Resolution**:
- **Primary split**: Checkpoints are valid ✅
- **Expanded splits**: Checkpoints have seen future test data ⚠️
- **Honest disclosure**: Current results use primary split only. Expanded_3yr_test requires retraining on 2015-2020.

### Risk 2: Normalization Leakage

**Problem**: Scalers fit on all data including test years.

**Resolution**: All normalization uses **train-only statistics**:
- `train_only_metadata_v1.json` stores q95, mean, std computed from train windows only
- Verified in `scripts/run_a3_flowmatching_augment.py` — uses per-sample history normalization
- Verified in `src/tidetail/total_water_eval.py` — scalers are passed from train split

### Risk 3: Threshold Selection

**Problem**: POT q95 threshold might be tuned on full data.

**Resolution**: POT q95 is computed from **residual data ≤ 2022** (train years only). Documented in `scripts/make_multiyear_cohort.py`.

### Risk 4: Augmentation Ratio Selection

**Problem**: Choosing best augmentation ratio based on held-out test performance.

**Resolution**: Wave 1.7 audit (WAVE17_INDEPENDENCE_AUDIT.md) verifies:
- All 3 augmentation ratios (10%, 25%, 50%) and both FM seeds (42, 99) are reported
- No "best ratio" selection on test data
- "Best per region" table correctly labeled as post-hoc

1. **Use primary split for current publication results** — checkpoints are valid, charleston is statistically significant.
2. **Disclose expanded split as future work** — needs retraining, 20-40 GPU hours.
3. **Never claim "fully pre-specified" for results** — split was defined after Wave 1.6 exploratory analysis.
