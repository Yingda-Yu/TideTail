# TideTail

**Extreme-Aware Diffusion Augmentation for Coastal Water-Level Forecasting**

TideTail investigates whether conditional diffusion-generated rare extreme coastal water-level events improve forecasting on independent real storm events. This is a hypothesis, not an established finding.

## Scientific goals
- Reliable event-grouped and chronological splits without cross-window leakage.
- Strong real-only, oversampling, perturbation and standard conditional diffusion baselines.
- Tail-aware generative augmentation, with optional physical and multi-gauge coherence.
- Evaluation on held-out real events: overall and tail errors, peaks, threshold detection and event-level confidence intervals.

## Research integrity
No synthetic data in validation or test. No future meteorological information unavailable at forecast issuance. Dataset licensing and gauge datums must be audited. Do not report unrun experiments, fabricated citations or assumed gains.

## Status
Initial research scaffolding; no validated empirical results.
